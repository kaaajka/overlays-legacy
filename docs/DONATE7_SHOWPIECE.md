## Why these primitives

MorphSVG solves continuous authored shape correspondence; DrawSVG controls how much of the thread is exposed. SplitText gives letter-level folding without image replacement. The camera uses GSAP rather than a competing animation system. Pixi's custom filter interface supports the chapter-specific curved seam matte; a generic blur/glitch preset cannot express that mask. This shader is an alpha composite, not particle simulation or invisible obstacle avoidance. Original music, artwork and accepted centered composition remain the authority.

References: [MorphSVG](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/MorphSVGPlugin/), [Pixi custom filter API](https://pixijs.com/8.x/guides/components/filters).
